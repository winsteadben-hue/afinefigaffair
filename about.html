About the Author

Teri Viduna

Teri Viduna has spent a lifetime combining creativity, communication, and design. With degrees in Journalism and Graphic Design, she built a successful business career while never losing sight of her dream of becoming an author.

After retiring from the business world, Teri dedicated herself to what she loves most — writing and creating books that inspire, entertain, educate, and bring people together. Her work reflects a wide range of interests, including friendship, family, creativity, coloring books, hobbies, travel, cooking, and the simple joys that make life meaningful.

Drawing on her background in storytelling, design, and publishing, Teri creates books that are both visually engaging and enjoyable to read. Whether she's sharing heartfelt reflections, practical guides, whimsical adventures, or creative projects, her goal is always the same: to encourage people to laugh more, connect more deeply, and celebrate everyday moments.

When she's not writing, Teri enjoys spending time with family and friends, creating new projects, exploring creative pursuits, and turning ideas into books.

All of Teri's books are available for purchase on Amazon.
